Professional Journey

My career spans more than two decades in enterprise technology, where I've specialized in integration engineering, system architecture, and digital transformation. Currently based in Frankfurt am Main, Germany, I work with clients across Europe, delivering solutions that combine technical excellence with strategic thinking.

  • 25+ years in Enterprise IT - Practical experience across diverse industries and technologies
